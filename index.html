Slow, calm documentaries to learn by and drift off to.

Each episode is a long, unhurried journey through one subject: the planets and stars, the deep past, the natural world. Gentle narration, soft music, subtitles and simple illustrations, with nothing sudden or loud.

Every script is researched from reliable sources, and the sources are listed in each video's description.

Night Notebook Studio

Night Notebook Studio is the private production tool behind the channel. It is used only by the channel's owner to prepare episodes and to publish them to the channel's own YouTube account through YouTube API Services. It is not offered to other people and has no public sign-up.

When connected to the channel's YouTube account, it uploads the channel's own videos with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules, and reads the channel's own performance statistics to plan future episodes. Uploading is always a separate, clearly labelled YouTube action started by the channel owner.
