Privacy policy

Night Notebook Studio · Effective 16 September 2026

Who this covers

Night Notebook Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Night Notebook to produce and publish the channel's own videos. It is not available to the public. This policy explains what the app does with data it obtains from YouTube API Services.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • The channel owner's own account, with permission (OAuth): uploading the channel's videos and setting their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, privacy status and publish times; reading the channel's own videos and their statistics (such as views and watch time) through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.
  • Public information, without access to anyone's account: public details of videos and channels on YouTube (such as titles, durations, publish dates and public view counts), used to research which calm documentary subjects people watch.

The app does not access, collect or store data about other YouTube users' accounts, comments, subscribers or personal information.

How data is stored and for how long

  • Access tokens for the channel owner's account are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared.
  • Data obtained from YouTube API Services is stored only as long as it is needed and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • The app keeps its own records of the channel's episodes (for example which video was published when) and summary figures it calculates itself.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, does not show advertising and does not use cookies or tracking. This website does not collect any personal information.

Revoking access

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
